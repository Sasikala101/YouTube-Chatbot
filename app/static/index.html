Load a public YouTube video with English captions, read its AI summary, and ask questions grounded in the transcript. Open the source timestamps to explore an idea in context.

How it works

We retrieve captions, split them into overlapping passages, and find passages matching your question. Gemini uses those passages to generate an answer. Current retrieval uses keywords, so specific terms from the video work best.

Your data

The transcript and your questions are sent to Google for AI generation. Recent video links stay in your browser. Server sessions expire after an hour or a restart.
